Questions

Who pays, and who can use it?

One adult subscribes and the whole household is covered: co-parents, kids' Sidekick profiles and any shared screens.

Do my kids need their own phone?

No. Kids can use a shared iPad in the kitchen, or their own device with a Sidekick profile you control.

What are Poppin AI tokens?

Each thing you ask Poppin AI to do uses a few tokens. Your plan refills them every month, and extra packs never expire.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel during the 7 days and you pay nothing.

What about our privacy?

Your household's data stays in your household. We don't sell it or use it for ads. Read the privacy policy.
